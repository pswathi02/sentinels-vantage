The solution

A temporal knowledge graph of any company

  • Type a domain → multi-source ingest fires across the open web
  • Drag the time slider (30 / 90 / 180 days) and the graph mutates live
  • Hit play → watch a company's collapse or breakout unfold week-by-week
  • Every node & fact is cited to the exact scraped article URL
  • Auto-memo with risk narrative + IC questions, export to PDF
Bitemporal model: every fact carries valid_from / valid_to / observed_at / source — that's what makes "what did this look like at time t?" possible.

03 / 06
Why it's different

Nobody treats the open web as a time-aware database

Incumbents read internal data rooms & pre-timestamped filings. We turn the messy public web into a queryable timeline.

🗂️ Hebbia · Rogo · AlphaSense

Operate on internal data rooms and already-timestamped SEC filings. They answer "what's in these documents?" — and the open web stays invisible.

⏳ Vantage

Structures the live public web into a bitemporal graph you can scrub through time. We answer "how did this company change, and when?"

⏱️ The time dimension is the moat

  • Trajectory > snapshot — two senior exits in 90 days is a distress signal a single snapshot can't see
  • Lead/lag patterns — a CMO departure often precedes a sentiment dip ~45 days later; we make that visible
  • Replayable history — drag back and watch Glassdoor stars climb, hiring expand, then the collapse animate forward
  • Provenance over time — every edge knows when it became true and where it came from
