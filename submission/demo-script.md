# Vantage — Demo Script (~4.5 min)

**Team Sentinels · Web Data Unlocked Hackathon · Track 2**
Pacing note: read at a relaxed ~150 words/min. **[bracketed]** = what to do on screen.

---

### 0:00 — Hook (15s)
"Before a fund writes a check, an analyst spends *forty hours* pulling LinkedIn, Glassdoor, news, filings and lawsuits — just to answer one question: *what's really going on with this company, and is it getting better or worse?*

We built **Vantage** — it does that in about ten seconds, and it lets you scrub backwards through time to watch the story unfold."

---

### 0:15 — The big idea (25s)
"Every tool today — Hebbia, AlphaSense, Rogo — reads *internal* data rooms and pre-timestamped filings. **Nobody treats the open web as a structured, time-aware database.**

Vantage does. We use **Bright Data** to continuously capture public signals, structure every fact with its date and its source, and lay it on a draggable timeline. So you don't just see a snapshot — you see the *trajectory*."

---

### 0:40 — Landing + kick off a target (20s)
**[Show the home screen. Hover the three demo cards.]**

"We've got three live examples — a distress arc, a breakout, and a mixed signal. Let's open **Spirit Airlines** — a textbook distress story.

**[Click spirit.com.]**

While this loads — and notice it teaches you something while you wait — every fact you're about to see was scraped from the real open web and cited back to its source."

---

### 1:00 — The knowledge graph (35s)
**[Dashboard loads. Point at the graph.]**

"This is the company's knowledge graph. The center is Spirit; around it are the people, lawsuits, filings, and partners we extracted. **Claude** read each scraped article and pulled out structured entities and relationships — every node here is backed by a real source.

**[Hover a node.]**

Hover any node and you get the exact quote and the link to the article it came from. No unsourced claims — that's the whole point."

---

### 1:35 — The time slider — the killer feature (45s)
**[Grab the time slider, drag it back.]**

"Here's where it gets fun. This is a *time machine*. As I drag the slider back, the graph **rebuilds itself** to how the company looked at that date.

**[Drag back, then hit play.]**

Watch — the CEO reappears, the bankruptcy filing disappears, the lawsuits roll back. Hit play and you watch the entire collapse unfold week by week.

This is the insight you can't get from a snapshot: *two senior exits inside ninety days, a lawsuit spike right after a leadership shake-up* — patterns that only show up when you can see them on a timeline."

---

### 2:20 — Signals & the delta report (40s)
**[Switch to the Timeline / Risk tab.]**

"Every signal is scored across six risk dimensions — management, legal, financial, culture, market, customer — and, importantly, the *direction* is correct: a new CEO joining is a positive signal, a resignation is negative. We don't just match keywords; we read the meaning.

**[Show the Δ Delta report.]**

The **Delta report** answers 'what changed in this window?' in plain English — new departures, new litigation, pricing moves — each line cited. And every signal here was *published within your selected window*, dated to when it actually happened — not when we scraped it."

---

### 3:00 — Ask + memo (35s)
**[Switch to the Ask / Memo tab. Type a question.]**

"You can ask it questions — *'why is leadership unstable?'* — and it answers with citations that point straight back to the graph.

**[Show the memo.]**

And it auto-generates a **diligence memo** — timeline, risk narrative, and the questions you'd take into an investment committee — exportable to PDF. The forty-hour first pass, done before your coffee's cold."

---

### 3:35 — Why Bright Data (30s)
"None of this works without **Bright Data**. Glassdoor, LinkedIn, G2 — these block scrapers within days. Bright Data's Web Unlocker and residential proxies keep the pipeline alive, the SERP API pulls news and litigation, and Datasets let us backfill ninety days of history on day one. One MCP interface, the entire open web. Without it, this collapses to a static snapshot."

---

### 4:05 — Close (20s)
"So that's **Vantage** — type a domain, and get a cited, time-aware diligence briefing in seconds instead of weeks.

We turn the messy public web into a database you can scrub through time. **Team Sentinels.** Thanks for watching."

**[End on the graph or the landing page.]**
